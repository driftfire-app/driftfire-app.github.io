Asteroid terrain gallery

Every match drops you into a drifting asteroid field. You flick — swipe to launch your ship — through the rock, and your shots bore holes clean through it. These are the field types you can choose in Settings → Asteroids → Method.

Each type shows its default look. The Part of random set types also vary their generation settings from match to match, so they add a few of those variations.

Caves

Part of random set

Mixed rock: round blobs and long ridges, each big asteroid hollowed by a ship-sized cave you can fly inside. The default — the most varied terrain to fight through.

Disc (round)

Plain round blobs scattered across open space — the original look, with little terrain to hide behind.

Noise

Soft lobed blobs from warped noise. Some fields come out fairly plain and open.

Craggy (fractal)

One jagged, craggy coastline of rock rather than separate asteroids.

Solid band

A single solid slab across the middle. Bore a hole to punch through, or loop around the open ends.

Breakout

A regular grid of small brick-like rocks across the middle, like the wall in Breakout.

Samples are generated straight from the game's field generator, so they track the real thing. Seeds: 1001 (default), 2002, 3003, 4004.
